# Feasibility assessment

## Decision

Technically feasible as a staged evidence intake and verification product. Not feasible to claim official police submission, formal complaint status, admissible evidence, or genuine physical location without specific agreements, legal review, operational controls, and device tests.

Gov.cy already exposes an anonymous information/complaint route for Cyprus Police. A standalone product therefore needs an agreed routing model or a narrower gap (for example, evidence packaging and verifiable receipt), rather than a duplicate unofficial police front door.

## Build independently

- Citizen-facing bilingual form and safety guidance.
- Evidence bundle format and offline hash/signature verifier.
- Capture metadata and explicit confidence states.
- Internal audit/event chain and synthetic test fixtures.
- Draft authority review portal using synthetic data only.

## Requires institution / legal gate

- Official Police, municipality, or agency intake and status integration.
- Representation as an official complaint or replacement for station attendance.
- Collection and processing roles, lawful basis, data-sharing, retention, disclosure, and data-subject handling.
- Any operational use of evidence to issue fines or initiate proceedings.
- A trusted timestamp service contract and an accepted signer identity.

## Product models

| Model | Feasibility | Main gate |
|---|---|---|
| Independent civic reporting | Buildable as a private evidence-preparation tool; users submit through confirmed official channels | No promise that upload equals official filing; avoid duplicate/confusing intake |
| Government-integrated | Best route for a genuine police substitute | Formal authority sponsor, lawful basis, security accreditation, integration agreement |
| White-label public sector | Viable for municipalities or agencies with defined mandates | Separate agreements, routing rules, controller/processor roles per agency |

## Current recommendation

Build an independent verifier and evidence-package prototype first. In parallel, ask relevant authorities whether a defined report type has a real intake gap. Keep formal reporting, routing and live pilot disabled until that answer and legal/privacy gates are documented.
