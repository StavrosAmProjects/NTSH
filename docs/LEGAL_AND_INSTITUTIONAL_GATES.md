# Legal and institutional gates

This is an issue list, not legal advice or a certified Cyprus legal opinion. Broad legal research remains out of scope until a specific implementation question requires it. No production processing or official representation should begin before qualified review.

1. **Existing channel and gap:** Gov.cy currently offers anonymous police information/complaint submission. Confirm which incident categories it covers and what the submitter receives. Avoid implying this app replaces the channel.
2. **Submission effect:** Determine whether a private intermediary may forward a report, and when that forwarding counts as receipt, complaint, statement, or merely information.
3. **Controller roles and lawful basis:** Identify controller/processor roles between operator, Police, municipalities, and vendors for alleged-offence data, images, location, device IDs, and attestation signals.
4. **Criminal-offence data:** Confirm GDPR Article 10 and, where competent authorities process law-enforcement data, Law 125(I)/2018 / Directive (EU) 2016/680 boundaries and actual lawful bases.
5. **Image/location capture:** Define lawful and proportionate capture rules for public/private spaces, bystanders, children, homes, and vehicle plates; establish redaction and disclosure rules.
6. **Evidence procedure:** Confirm Cyprus criminal and administrative evidence rules, original-file handling, witness/foundation needs, expert verification requirements, and whether remote/anonymous reports can support a proceeding.
7. **Timestamps and signatures:** Confirm exact eIDAS effect for qualified timestamps/signatures and whether any authority or procedure requires them. Do not claim admissibility from eIDAS status alone.
8. **DPIA and rights:** Complete DPIA, retention schedule, legal holds, access/correction/restriction workflow, and safeguards for persons named or depicted.
9. **Authority routing:** Establish each category's competent agency, agreement, secure endpoint, service levels, acknowledgment, error handling, and audit access.
10. **AI:** Keep AI out of guilt/fine decisions. If AI is later added, assess Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 scope, role, classification, transparency and human oversight.

## Gate status

All items above: **BLOCKED pending targeted Cyprus counsel and authority confirmation**. The existence of an online anonymous reporting channel is verified from Gov.cy; scope and legal effects for this proposed product are not verified.
